About the Olympiad

What is BBO 3.0?

A national-level competition promoting scientific excellence and modern bioinformatics.

The Flagship Event

The Biology & Bioinformatics Olympiad 3.0 is a national-level competition organized by BioPC. Following the tremendous success of the previous edition, which attracted nearly 3,000 participants from universities across Bangladesh, BioPC is proud to launch the third edition.

The Olympiad aims to inspire students in the life sciences, promote scientific excellence, and provide a platform for exploring modern biology and bioinformatics through competitive learning and problem-solving.

Biology & Bioinformatics Olympiad 3.0 - Syllabus

Free, fully online screening round conducted through Google Meet, TestMoz, and LiveMCQ.

Subjects: Botany and Zoology, at a level suited to early-year undergraduate students.

Exam date: 31 July 2026

Advanced round held on campus at University of Chittagong (CU), for participants who qualify through Round 1.

Date: To be announced later.

Basic Biochemistry: Biomolecules (Carbohydrates, Proteins, Lipids, Nucleic Acids), Enzymes & Enzyme Kinetics, Vitamins & Minerals, ATP & Bioenergetics, Protein Structure & Folding, Metabolism (Glycolysis, TCA Cycle, Electron Transport Chain), Hormonal Regulation, pH & Buffer Systems, Basic Human Nutrition.

Cell Biology: Cell Structure (Prokaryotic & Eukaryotic), Cell Organelles, Cell Membrane & Cellular Transport, Cell Cycle & Cell Division, Cytogenetics, Cell Signalling.

Molecular Biology & Genetics: DNA & RNA Structure, Central Dogma, DNA Replication, Transcription, Translation, Gene Expression & Regulation, Chromatin, Nucleosomes & Histones, DNA Repair Mechanisms, Mutations, Genetic Code, PCR, Mendelian Genetics, Linkage & Crossing Over.

Genetic Engineering & Biotechnology: Recombinant DNA Technology, Gene Cloning, Vectors (Plasmid & Viral), Restriction Enzymes & Ligase, Transformation, Transgenic Organisms, CRISPR-Cas9, Gene Therapy, GMOs & Biosafety, Tissue Culture, Protein Engineering, Synthetic Biology, Vaccines, Industrial & Medical Biotechnology.

Microbiology & Immunology: General Microbiology, Bacteriology, Virology, Algology, Mycology, Parasitology, Microbial Diseases, Sterilisation & Disinfection, Antibiotic Resistance, Antigen-Antibody Interaction, Humoral Immunity.

Bioinformatics & Applied AI: Introduction to Bioinformatics, Biological Databases (NCBI, ENSEMBL, PubMed), DNA/RNA/Protein Sequence Analysis, Sequence Alignment, FASTA, BLAST, Gene Expression Analysis, Protein Structure & Interaction, Primer Design, Basics of Programming, Machine Learning Basics, Data Analysis & Biostatistics, AI Applications in Healthcare & Drug Discovery, Bioethics.

Pharmacy: Pharmacodynamics, Pharmacokinetics, Drug Action Mechanisms, Drug Development, Toxicology, Basic Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Common Diseases & Symptoms.

Botany: Plant Physiology, Plant Molecular Biology, Plant Biochemistry, Plant Diseases, Plant Anatomy, Plant Morphology, Plant Reproduction, Plant Taxonomy, Evolution of Plants (Algae, Bryophyte, Pteridophytes, Gymnosperms, Angiosperms), Economic Botany, Plant Ecology, Forestry and Wildlife Resources of Bangladesh.

Zoology: Digestive System, Blood & Circulatory System, Respiratory System, Kidney & Excretory System, Nervous System, Reproductive System, Immune System, Endocrine System, Locomotion and Organ Movement, Common Human Diseases, Taxonomy, Evolution, Animal Behavior.

Chemistry: Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonding, Organic Chemistry & Functional Groups, Acids & Bases, Solutions, Gases & Liquids, Analytical Chemistry.

Agriculture & Environmental Science: Plant Growth & Development, Soil Science, Crop Science and Crop Improvement, Agricultural Biotechnology, Ecosystem & Biodiversity, Conservation, Climate Change, Environmental Pollution and Monitoring.

Public Health: Determinants of Health, Epidemiology (Incidence, Prevalence, Morbidity, Mortality), Communicable & Non-Communicable Diseases, Disease Prevention (Primary, Secondary & Tertiary) and Screening, Nutrition & Nutritional Deficiencies, Water, Sanitation & Hygiene (WASH), Environmental Health, Maternal & Child Health, Mental Health, One Health.

Fisheries: Fish Biology, Fish Physiology, Aquaculture, Fish Nutrition, Fish Diseases & Health Management, Water Quality Management, Fish Ecology, Fisheries Management, Fish Genetics & Breeding, Aquatic Biodiversity & Conservation, Capture Fisheries & Fishing Gear, Fish Processing & Post-Harvest Technology, Fisheries Resources of Bangladesh.

Scientific History & General Knowledge: History of Biology, Major Scientific Discoveries, Famous Scientists, Nobel Prizes in Life Sciences, Important Inventions, Scientific Organizations, Current Scientific Affairs.

Common FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the 3rd Biology & Bioinformatics Olympiad.

1 Is there any registration fee?

Online round is completely free. If you qualify for the second round, you will have to pay a little fees for offline program support.

2 What is the registration deadline?

The registration deadline is 29 July 2026 at 11:59 PM (BST).

3 Which platforms will be used for the online round?

The online round will be conducted through Google Meet, TestMoz, and LiveMCQ.

4 Where will the final round take place?

The final round will be held at the University of Chittagong (CU).

5 What topics will be covered in the Olympiad?

Questions may come from Biology, Molecular Biology, Genetics, Biotechnology, Microbiology, Bioinformatics, Biochemistry, Zoology, Botany, Environmental Science, and related Life Science fields.

6 Will there be Bioinformatics questions for beginners?

Yes. The Olympiad will include both fundamental and advanced-level questions so students from different academic backgrounds can participate comfortably.

7 Is teamwork allowed?

No. The Olympiad is an individual competition.

8 How will participants be selected for the final round?

Participants with the highest scores in the online elimination round will qualify for the offline final round.

9 What should I prepare before the online round?

Participants should ensure a stable internet connection, a charged device, and access to the required platforms before the exam starts.

10 Can freshers or first-year students participate?

Absolutely. Students from all academic years are encouraged to join.
