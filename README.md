# Biology & Bioinformatics Olympiad 3.0 (2026) | BBO3.0

Official website for the **3rd Biology & Bioinformatics Olympiad** organized by **BioPC** — A Bioinformatics Lab of Research & Training.

## 🧬 About BBO 3.0

The Biology & Bioinformatics Olympiad 3.0 is a national-level competition dedicated to:
- **Promoting scientific excellence** in biology and bioinformatics
- **Inspiring students** in the life sciences
- **Providing a platform** for exploring modern biology through competition
- **Connecting talent** with research and internship opportunities

### Key Details
- **Event**: National Level Competition
- **Registration**: Completely **FREE**
- **Format**: Hybrid (Online + Campus Round)
- **Expected Participants**: 3000+
- **Organizing Body**: BioPC Lab
