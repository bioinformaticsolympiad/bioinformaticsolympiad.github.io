Round 2 · Offline · University of Chittagong

You're through to the final round

Round 1 was written from home. Round 2 brings everyone into one hall at the University of Chittagong — a full day of examination, presentation and workshop built around the people who made it this far.

—

Why the final round is held in person

An online MCQ can measure recall. It cannot watch you defend a poster, think on your feet, or meet the person who becomes your research collaborator. Round 2 exists for the things a browser cannot host.

🏆

Prizes for every segment

Awards for the top performers in the examination, the poster presentation, the photography contest and the instant speech.

🥇

Crest & certificates

A crest for the winners and an individually issued certificate for every participant — valid for your CV and scholarship applications.

🔬

Hands-on workshop

A practical bioinformatics workshop run on the day, using the tools working researchers actually use.

💼

Internship opportunities

Standout participants are considered for offline internship placements with BioPC — the route several of our current team took.

📘

Book & study materials

Curated reading to take home, so the day continues to pay off long after you leave the hall.

🍽️

Lunch & snacks included

Lunch and refreshments are provided for the full day. No queueing, no wandering off campus to find food.

🤝

Collaboration & network

Meet students from over 250 institutions, plus faculty and researchers. Research partnerships start in rooms like this one.

🎤

Communication skills

Presenting your work and speaking without a script are skills no exam teaches. Both are on the programme.

Registration fee — and where it goes

The Olympiad is run by a small team, not a sponsor's budget. The fee covers the day itself: hall and equipment, printed certificates and crests, workshop materials, lunch and snacks for every attendee, and the take-home book. Nothing in it is profit.

Only ৳100 more opens three additional competitive segments, each with its own prize and certificate. If you are travelling to Chittagong for the day anyway, it is the better value.
