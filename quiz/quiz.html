Timed Quiz Examination

Decentralized individual countdown starts the exact moment you enter. Closing or reloading the tab continues the clock in real-time.

Kahoot-Style Live Speed Bonus

Each MCQ tracks your response time with a 60-second mathematical decay. Answering accurately in 5s awards ~917 bonus points; 30s awards ~500 bonus points (only for correct answers!).

Weighted Tie-Breaker Ranking

Base accuracy dominates: 1 mark per correct answer (Max: 10.0). Speed points break ties among equal scorers on the live leaderboard.

Chrome "Ask Gemini" & AI Strictly Prohibited

Interacting with Chrome's "Ask Gemini" sidebar, copying questions, right-clicking to ask AI, or leaving the window is strictly prohibited. Violations immediately terminate and lock your exam, requiring BioPC Administrator authorization for a 2nd attempt.

Strict Linear Order & 5s Auto-Advance

Questions must be answered sequentially one-by-one. When you select an answer, your points & speed bonus are revealed immediately, and after 5 seconds it automatically advances to the next question (or you can click to advance manually at any time!).

Keyboard Accessibility

Speed up your responses by pressing keys A, B, C, D to select options and ← / → to navigate.
