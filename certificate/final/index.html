Who receives a certificate

  • Certificate of Excellence. The top five places, awarded as winners.
  • Certificate of Merit. Places 6 to 15, awarded Special Recognition.
  • Certificate of Participation. Everyone else who passed, carrying their placing and score.

Certificates are issued only to candidates who passed the Final Round, which is 40% or more. Every certificate shows a placing, a score and a unique ID.
