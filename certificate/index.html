Generate your certificate

Every qualified participant of Round 1 can download a personalised Certificate of Participation carrying their own name, rank and unique ID.

How to get yours

  1. Check your result. Open the result checker and enter the email address you registered with.
  2. Scroll down and press “Generate Your Certificate”. The button appears near the bottom of your result once it is verified.
  3. Wait on the page. A small notice appears in the corner while your certificate is prepared.
  4. It downloads by itself as a PNG image, named after you and your unique ID.

Your certificate is built from your verified result, so it cannot be generated without checking your result first. That is what keeps every certificate genuine.
